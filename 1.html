Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked OEM Questions

Key information regarding MOQ, sample policies, and production turnaround.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom private label?

Generally, one 20GP or 40HQ container per SKU to optimize high-speed servo line setup and printed bag manufacturing costs. Trial configurations can be negotiated.

Can we request free prototypes and quality samples?

Yes. We provide sample diapers and pads free of charge to confirm absorbency, fit, and softness. The client only covers express courier fees.

Do you support custom printing on backsheets and polybags?

Yes. Our in-house design team assists with flexographic and rotogravure plate designs for wetness indicators, front tapes, and multi-color retail bags.

Which export certifications are available for international customs?

Tiico facilities operate under ISO 9001:2015 and maintain valid US FDA facility registration, CE medical device declarations, and FSC timber traceability certification.
